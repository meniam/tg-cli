    Moves records of past months from the root of .agents/changelog/ into
    YYYY-MM/ subfolders. The current month stays in the root. File contents do
    not change.

  What runs
    1. uv run bin/changelog.py archive [--dry-run]
      --dry-run   only show how many files go to which months

  When to run
    At the start of a new month, in a separate commit.

  Examples
    just changelog archive --dry-run
    just changelog archive
