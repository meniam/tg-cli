    Shows changelog records: newest first, under a heading per day. By default
    the last 7 days; the archive of past months is read too.

  What runs
    1. uv run bin/changelog.py list [--days N] [--all] [--type TYPE] [--breaking]
      --days N     the last N days instead of 7
      --all        every record of all time
      --type TYPE  one type: Добавлено, Изменено, Устарело, Удалено, Исправлено, Безопасность
      --breaking   incompatible changes only

  Examples
    just changelog list
    just changelog list --days 30 --type Исправлено
    just changelog list --all --breaking
